# Link prospect qualification

## Prospect types

- resource or tools page
- editorial article
- comparison or alternatives page
- relevant directory or association
- partner or integration page
- institution, library, or educational resource
- journalist, publisher, or research citation opportunity
- broken or outdated reference replacement
- unlinked brand or asset mention

## Qualification checks

1. Is the prospect page live and indexable?
2. Is its audience relevant to the asset?
3. Does the page already link to external resources or cite sources?
4. Is there a concrete placement or editorial update to propose?
5. Is the asset materially useful, accurate, and current?
6. Are authority metrics measured and dated rather than assumed?
7. Is the site free from obvious spam, paid-link, or scraped-content patterns?
8. Can outreach be honest without inventing familiarity or urgency?

## Outreach guardrails

- Reference the exact page and a real reason the asset improves it.
- Keep the request optional and respectful.
- Do not make false praise, false scarcity, or unsupported claims.
- Do not claim the recipient's name or role unless verified.
- Never promise payment or reciprocal links unless the user explicitly defines a compliant partnership.
