# Backlink outreach templates and policy notes

Replace every bracketed field with verified prospect-specific information. Delete any sentence that does not apply. These are structures, not mail-merge copy.

## Editorial update or listicle replacement

Subject:

```text
Update for your [topic] list
```

Body:

```text
Hi [name],

Your [page title] still says [exact outdated or incorrect point]. [Brief source or checked fact] now shows [correction].

I [built/work on] [app], which [one-sentence function] for [specific user]. It may fit [exact section] because [reader-relevant difference]. I can send [small evidence package] so you can compare it with the current entries.

Would a [two-sentence entry/screenshot/current pricing note] help with the update?

[real signature and required compliance footer]
```

Do not call an entry outdated merely because the sender wants inclusion. If the current item still fits, pitch the app as an additional option and explain the distinct audience.

## Guest contribution

Subject:

```text
Guest idea: [specific reader outcome]
```

Body:

```text
Hi [name],

I noticed [site] covers [specific topic] but not [documented gap]. I can contribute a practical piece on [proposed title or angle] for [reader], based on [real experience, data, or workflow].

The article would cover [two or three concrete sections]. I [built/work on] [app], so I would disclose that relationship and keep any product reference relevant to the explanation.

Is the topic suitable for your contributor calendar? I can send a short outline first.

[real signature and required compliance footer]
```

Follow the site's contributor rules. Do not demand a followed link, optimized anchor, or publication date.

## Paid guest post or sponsorship

Subject:

```text
Sponsorship rates for [page or section]
```

Body:

```text
Hi [name],

I'm looking at a disclosed sponsorship around [specific topic/page] for [app], which [verified function] for [audience]. The fit is [one specific reason].

Our budget is [amount and currency/range]. We are comfortable with clear sponsorship disclosure and rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow". We can provide [creative, draft, data, or product access], subject to your editorial review.

Could you send the relevant rates, audience details, and placement terms?

[real signature and required compliance footer]
```

Payment must not depend on a followed link, positive review, false statement, or hidden sponsorship.

## Narrow reciprocal resource partnership

Subject:

```text
Resource fit for [shared topic]
```

Body:

```text
Hi [name],

Your [target page] explains [specific point]. I [built/work on] [app] ([app URL]), which helps [specific audience] [verified function]. Our [resource page] adds [specific evidence, tool, or example] that could help readers at [exact location].

We also maintain [sender page URL], where your [recipient resource] would help [sender audience] with [distinct need]. Our site currently has [verified DA or DR and dated traffic estimate, only if useful], and the audiences overlap around [topic].

Would you be open to reviewing both resources independently? Either editor should skip a link that does not fit the page.

[real signature and required compliance footer]
```

Do not use this template for scaled reciprocal-link campaigns. Each page must have its own editorial reason to link.

## Metric language

Good:

```text
Our site receives about 18,000 estimated organic visits a month according to Ahrefs, checked 12 August 2026.
Our Ahrefs Domain Rating is 31, checked 12 August 2026.
Our Moz Domain Authority is 28, checked 12 August 2026.
```

Bad:

```text
We have a DA of 31.
```

The bad version is unusable if 31 is actually DR, the source is unknown, or the date is stale.

## Search policy

Google classifies buying or selling links for ranking purposes and excessive reciprocal linking as link spam. Paid links are acceptable as advertising or sponsorship when they are qualified with `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`:

- <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies>
- <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links>

Do not promise that a link will pass ranking credit or improve positions.

## Commercial email policy

- US CAN-SPAM guidance covers business-to-business commercial email and requires accurate sender and subject information, identification, postal address, opt-out, and suppression: <https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business>
- UK ICO guidance says publicly available contact details do not by themselves create marketing consent and explains different rules for corporate and individual subscribers: <https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/>
- Canadian CASL guidance explains consent, identification, unsubscribe, relevance, and the limited role of conspicuous publication: <https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm>
- Gmail requires sender authentication and adds unsubscribe and spam-rate requirements for bulk promotional email: <https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126>

The sender must apply the rules for the actual jurisdictions and sending volume. A template cannot supply legal permission.
