# Competitive landscape framework

## Query lanes

- category and head terms
- high-intent product or service searches
- problems and use cases
- alternatives and comparisons
- informational questions
- local or industry modifiers when relevant

## Domain classification

For each recurring domain, record:

| Domain | Competitor type | Query lanes | Recurrence | Winning page types | Authority evidence | Strategic relevance | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Pattern categories

- information architecture and topic hubs
- page formats and content depth
- original data, tools, templates, or community content
- freshness and update cadence
- authorship, citations, policies, and entity clarity
- internal linking and navigational prominence
- backlinks and referring-domain quality when measured
- search-result features and intent shifts

## Opportunity test

Call something an opportunity only when:

1. it fits the user's business and audience
2. the query set shows a real task or demand signal
3. current results leave a meaningful weakness or underserved angle
4. the user can plausibly produce a more useful page or asset

Label speculative opportunities and the evidence needed to validate them.
