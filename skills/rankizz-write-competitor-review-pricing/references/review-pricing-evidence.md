# Review and pricing evidence standards

Research volatile product and pricing facts again immediately before publication. Official sources establish stated prices, capabilities, and terms. First-hand testing must describe work that was actually completed.

## Working rules

- Explain what was tested, provide evidence of experience when it exists, measure where useful, discuss benefits and drawbacks, compare alternatives, and explain what distinguishes the product.
- Do not invent hands-on experience, reproduce another publisher's judgment, or disguise commercial content as independent editorial work.
- Keep affiliate, free-access, sponsorship, ownership, and competitor disclosures clear and close to the recommendation.
- Avoid paying for or soliciting reviews conditioned on positive sentiment.
- Structured data must represent visible, eligible content and comply with current rules. Do not add a self-serving rating merely to seek a review snippet.
