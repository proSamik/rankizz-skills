## Organic footprint

- estimated traffic and keyword breadth, labeled as estimates
- ranking distribution and intent mix
- strongest URLs and topic clusters
- branded versus non-branded visibility where distinguishable
- markets and languages actually covered

## Content and experience

- information architecture and page types
- depth, freshness, authorship, citations, and original evidence
- tools, templates, interactive features, or community value
- conversion paths and product integration
- structured data and entity clarity

## Authority

- referring-domain quality and relevance when measured
- recurring linkable assets or digital PR patterns
- institutional, partner, community, or primary-source signals
- brand visibility that cannot be reduced to backlinks

## Strategic conclusion

Separate:

- **Learn from:** a pattern supported by evidence.
- **Avoid:** a tactic that does not fit the user or carries risk.
- **Differentiate:** a stronger angle rooted in the user's advantages.
- **Validate:** an inference needing more data.
