## Diagnosis

State:

- authority band
- traction stage
- primary constraint
- secondary constraint
- proof and peer SEO competitors
- chosen growth branch

## Roadmap table

| When | Priority | Action | Owner | Input/dependency | Deliverable | Acceptance criterion | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Every action must fit the duration and known team capacity. Separate actions the agent can perform from changes requiring user approval or engineering work.

## Opportunity queue

For every proposed page include:

- keyword and intent
- actual volume and KD when available
- selected page type
- product-led tool or editorial label
- business relevance
- proof/peer domains observed in the top 20
- target existing URL or proposed slug
- evidence and confidence

Do not aggregate duplicate close-variant volume as independent demand.

## Backlink workstream

Include:

- current referring-domain baseline
- relevant gap to proof/peer competitors when measured
- controllable asset, prospecting, reclaim, and outreach goals
- desired relevant referring-domain outcome as a range, not a promise
- unsafe tactics to avoid

## Measurement

Separate:

- **Leading indicators:** pages shipped, fixes completed, internal links, qualified prospects, outreach replies, crawl/index status.
- **Search outcomes:** impressions, clicks, CTR, query/page positions as trends.
- **Business outcomes:** organic sessions, engagement, key events, transactions, revenue, or qualified leads.

Set the re-check date far enough for the action. Do not promise same-day indexing or ranking movement.

## Closing summary

End with:

1. the three highest-value actions
2. the single action to start today
3. the main risk or unknown
4. approvals needed
5. what not to do
