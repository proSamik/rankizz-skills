# Comparison-page evidence standards

Use product documentation, pricing, limits, policies, changelogs, and terms for current factual claims. Use independent evidence for experience claims. Apply the same standard to both products.

## Working rules

- Demonstrate expertise with original evidence, quantitative measurements where appropriate, and decision-relevant benefits and drawbacks.
- Compare directly with competitors when that helps the reader, but do not manufacture experience or conclusions.
- State commercial relationships clearly and close to affected recommendations.
- Do not produce reversed or near-duplicate comparison pages that add no original value.
- Structured data must match visible content and current eligibility rules. Eligibility never guarantees a search feature.
