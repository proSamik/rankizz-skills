Use these sources to resolve policy or quality questions. Do not copy their language into the draft.

- Google people-first content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Google guidance for generative AI content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content

- Digital.gov plain-language principles: https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/principles

- FTC affiliate and endorsement guidance: https://consumer.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking

## Working rules

- Optimize for the reader's successful outcome, not a word count or keyword density.
- Make authorship, expertise, method, and automation context visible when readers would reasonably care.
- Prefer original reporting, data, examples, and analysis over summaries of existing pages.
- Use active voice and familiar terms unless the audience expects technical language.
- Treat automated mass production without added value as a stop condition.
- Place commercial disclosures where readers encounter the recommendation or link.
