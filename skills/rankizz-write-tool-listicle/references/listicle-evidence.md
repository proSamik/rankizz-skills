# Listicle evidence standards

Use current primary sources when ranking products. These pages establish the minimum quality and disclosure principles:

- Google's guidance for high-quality reviews: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews
- Google's people-first content guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Google's spam policies, including thin affiliation and scaled content abuse: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- Google's rules for qualifying paid links: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links
- FTC affiliate disclosure guidance: https://consumer.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- FTC guidance against pay-to-play comparison placement: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/soliciting-paying-online-reviews-guide-marketers

## Minimum evidence for an entry

- a clear fit for the stated audience
- current official capability and pricing sources
- a real differentiator or decision reason
- a concrete drawback
- transparent testing status
- commercial relationship disclosure

Exclude an entry when its only support is popularity, an affiliate program, or repeated claims from other listicles.
