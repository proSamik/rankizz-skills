# Local site audit report

## Coverage and limitations

Document the target, date, crawl budget, pages fetched, robots behavior, skipped URLs, blocked requests, and unavailable evidence.

## Status summary

Use `critical`, `needs work`, `healthy`, or `not assessed` for:

- crawlability and indexability
- technical and on-page SEO
- content and entity clarity
- GEO readiness
- AEO readiness
- structured data
- performance and experience

Avoid opaque numeric scores. If the user asks for scoring, show a transparent rubric and list the evidence used for every deduction.

## Priorities

Select three supported actions using severity, breadth, business impact, confidence, and effort. Do not rank speculative findings above verified blockers.

## Pages reviewed

List the page type, status, indexability, title, main heading, and notable observation for each fetched URL.

## What works

Record evidence-backed strengths.

## Remediation sequence

Group work into immediate blockers, template fixes, content improvements, and later experiments.
