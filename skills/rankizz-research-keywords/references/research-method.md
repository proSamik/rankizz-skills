# Keyword research method

## Candidate lanes

Build candidates from several lanes so one source does not dominate:

1. category and product terms
2. problems and jobs to be done
3. use cases and audiences
4. alternatives and comparisons
5. questions, definitions, and processes
6. integrations, features, locations, or industries where relevant
7. existing impressions and ranking queries
8. competitor topics supported by independent evidence

## Intent labels

Use the smallest useful set:

- informational
- commercial investigation
- transactional
- navigational
- local

Add a journey stage or page type separately rather than creating dozens of overlapping intent labels.

## Prioritization

Rank with explicit reasoning instead of an unexplained composite score. Consider:

- relevance: direct, adjacent, weak
- conversion proximity: high, medium, low
- demand evidence: measured, directional, unknown
- competition evidence: strong, moderate, weak, unknown
- existing fit: matching page, partial page, no page, conflicting pages
- confidence: high, medium, low

## Exclusion reasons

Record why a term was removed: irrelevant meaning, wrong geography, branded-only, duplicate, impossible intent mismatch, no business fit, misleading claim, or insufficient evidence.
